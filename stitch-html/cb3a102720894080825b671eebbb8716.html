Clause 1.0 Statutory Compliance

1. Scope of Impartial Custodial Services & Escrow Segregation

1.1 Pactly Technologies Ltd ("the Custodian") provides independent escrow verification software for commercial agreements. Funds deposited by Brands or Agency counterparties are deposited into segregated, insolvency-remote client balances administered via FCA-regulated Electronic Money Institution partner rails (Stripe Payments UK, Ltd, FRN 900461) in strict compliance with the UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and Payment Services Regulations 2017.

1.2 At no point do client escrow funds form part of Pactly’s general corporate assets. Neither party’s liquidators, administrators, nor general judgment creditors shall hold right, title, or claim to ring-fenced funds prior to verified milestone execution or arbitrator award.

Clause 2.0 Execution Milestones

2. Automated Acceptance & Milestone Verification Triggers

2.1 Ghosting Mitigation Protocol: Upon delivery confirmation of designated digital campaign deliverables (including raw asset uploads or authenticated social permalinks), the commissioning Brand counterparty is granted a definitive inspection window of forty-eight (48) consecutive hours ("Inspection Period").

2.2 If the Brand counterparty fails to either: (a) formally approve the milestone, or (b) lodge a specific Non-Conforming Deliverable Notice via the Pactly interface within said 48 hours, the Custodial System will automatically trigger irrevocable disbursement of the secured escrow funds to the Creator counterparty's payout balance.

Clause 3.0 Licensing Rights

3. Intellectual Property Transfer & Whitelisting Licenses

3.1 All copyright, database rights, and digital personality permissions remain strictly vested in the Creator until final escrow release has been cleared by bank settlement. Whitelisting or paid advertising amplification rights granted under custom pact addenda take effect contemporaneously with custodial fund release.

3.2 Unauthorised organic reposting or commercial ad asset usage prior to verified escrow release constitutes a material breach of English Copyright Law (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988) actionable under expedited injunctive relief.

Clause 4.0 Arbitration & Dispute

4. Dispute Escalation, Evidence Packs & Solicitor Arbitration

4.1 In the event of an irreconcilable milestone dispute, either party may invoke the Pactly Independent Solicitor Referee Protocol. Both parties agree to submit digital evidence packs within seventy-two (72) hours.

4.2 The dispute shall be submitted to a panel solicitor admitted to practice in England and Wales. The arbitration fee is strictly capped at seventy-five pounds sterling (£75.00) per party. The arbitrator's written decision shall be binding, non-appealable, and enforce immediate partial or complete disbursement of the disputed escrow deposit.

Clause 5.0 Jurisdiction

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

5.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

5.2 By executing digital acceptance below, each counterparty consents to electronic execution under the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000 and warrants full legal authority to enter binding commercial arrangements.
